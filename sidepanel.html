Help and scoring
Kibitz accuracy is an engine-based estimate, not your rating or a prediction of your results. Provisional assessments can change after deeper analysis.
Core analysis runs locally. Optional cloud explanations send position evidence to the selected provider.
PGN
A text record of a chess game.
Evaluation
The engine's estimate of the position. Positive values favor White.
Variation
A possible sequence of moves, separate from what was played.
Pin
A piece cannot move without exposing a more valuable target; legal king safety must still be checked.
